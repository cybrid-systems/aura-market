# M6 — score parts

`mk:score-m0` is `pnl - pen_inv`. `mk:score` stays on that formula, so the
M0 tape is still tight `-5` and wide `18`. `mk:score-q` is
`pnl - pen_inv - pen_adv`.

`pen_inv` is `quotient(sum |inv|, 4) + 2·|inv_end|`. `pen_adv` adds one
on the tick after a fill when that tick's drift still hurts the inventory
the fill left (long and drift &lt; 0, or short and drift &gt; 0).

| part | value |
|------|------:|
| pnl | 89 |
| pen_inv | 71 |
| pen_adv | 3 |
| score-q | 15 |

`RACE` and `PANEL` print `pnl=`, `pen_inv=`, and `pen_adv=`. The parts are
a record of the first panel seed. KEEP still uses one integer, the panel
minimum of `mk:score-m0`.

`fat-px` rejects a quote more than 8 away from fair, after the older
reasons. Distance 8 stays `ok`. The order does not rest.

pnl is `40` and its score is `31`, above wide `18`, but the ask is

to `(3 5 0)`.
