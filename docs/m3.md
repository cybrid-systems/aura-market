Seed panel is fixed in `soft/market/panel.aura`. Horizon is 48, the same
as M0. `mk:score-seed` does not write the main book. The first seed is
the M0 tape `20261005`.

| seed | mm-wide | mm-tight |

| 20261005 | 18 | -5 |
| 1265 | 73 | -2 |
| 9 | 83 | 22 |

On this panel the minimum is the M0 seed for both quoters, so the M2
single-seed numbers stay the minimum: wide `18`, tight `-5`, and
`(3 3 0)` is `36`. Median is the toward-zero mean of the two central
scores. It is printed and is not the KEEP score.

| quoter | min | median |
|--------|-----|--------|
| mm-wide | 18 | 56 |
| mm-tight | -5 | 3 |

`(2 2 2)` scores `50` on `20261005` and its panel minimum is `-4`, so
the propose path DROPs it and heals. A win on the M0 seed is not a KEEP.

`bash scripts/smoke_m3.sh` prints `MARKET_M3_OK` and re-runs the M0, M1,
and M2 smokes.
